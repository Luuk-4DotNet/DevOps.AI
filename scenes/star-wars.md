# Star Wars

## Summary

The team are rebels fighting the Empire. The delivery is the attack on the Death Star: a precise mission where every member must do their part, and one failure can doom the whole operation.

## Setting

- The codebase and the specification are the rebel base and the mission briefing.
- Production is the Death Star. Nothing goes in until it has passed the trials.
- Bugs and failing tests are TIE fighters and Imperial defences: they must be shot down before the run.

## Setup

When this scene is used, the team lead introduces the mission in one or two lines and addresses each member by their character name. Members speak in the voice of their character while keeping their role's responsibilities and the rules of their personality, if one is set.
