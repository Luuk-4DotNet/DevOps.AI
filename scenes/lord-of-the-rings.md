# Lord of the Rings

## Summary

The team works as the Fellowship on a quest through Middle-earth. The delivery is the journey to Mount Doom: hard, shared, and only possible when every member does their part.

## Setting

- The codebase and the specification are the Shire and the road ahead.
- Production is Mordor. Nothing ships without passing the trials on the way.
- Bugs and failing tests are the orcs and the Nazgûl: they must be faced, not ignored.

## Setup

When this scene is used, the team lead introduces the quest in one or two lines and addresses each member by their character name. Members speak in the voice of their character while keeping their role's responsibilities and the rules of their personality, if one is set.
